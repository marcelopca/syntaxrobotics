Coreless Film

Film carriage can be fitted with a coreless mandrel. Achieves 100% film consumption.

Mechanical Stretch

Film carriage with mechanical brake, manually adjustable to set film tensioning. New switch-brake design makes it easier to adjust film tension.

Variable Pre-stretch

Film carriage is driven by two motors to operate the pre-stretching rollers. The pre-stretching rate can be adjusted from 0% to 500% through the screen.
